# Fusion 360 Design Portfolio

A comprehensive collection of mechanical design projects created in Autodesk Fusion 360, showcasing a range of designs from fundamental mechanical components to complex assemblies and mechanisms.

## Project Overview

This repository contains 30+ professional-grade 3D designs and assemblies, organized into three categories:

### 📐 Basic Designs
A foundational collection of 23 essential mechanical designs covering fundamental engineering principles and component geometry. These designs serve as building blocks for more complex assemblies.

**Contents:**
- Design 1-22: Core mechanical components and geometric studies
- Design 23: Advanced geometric variation

### 🔧 Advanced Designs
Sophisticated mechanical systems and assemblies demonstrating advanced engineering concepts and complex interactions between components.

**Featured Projects:**
- **Advance Design 1 & 2**: Specialized mechanical systems
- **Angular Transmitter Mechanism**: Power transmission system with angular offset capabilities
- **Ball Joint**: Spherical joint assembly for multi-directional movement
- **Cardan Joint**: Universal joint for torque transmission across non-parallel shafts
- **Engine - Piston & Crank Shaft**: Complete piston-crank mechanism demonstrating internal combustion engine principles
- **Geneva Wheel Mechanism**: Intermittent motion converter mechanism

### 🤖 Robot Assembly
Complete robotic system assembly combining multiple mechanical subsystems into an integrated unit.

**Contents:**
- **Robot Assemble**: Full-scale robotic assembly with integrated mechanical components

## Technical Details

- **Software**: Autodesk Fusion 360
